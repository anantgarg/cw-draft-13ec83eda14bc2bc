15:T1347,<p>A Crossword answer can now show a product card at the point in the reply where it belongs. The agent writes a lead-in, the card appears and the explanation continues underneath.</p>
<p>Before this work, every card was held back and shown only after the final reply text. That hold was deliberate. Two earlier fixes buffered cards until the reply was finished, so a card would not render above the answer or be mistaken for the run&#39;s reply. The models already produce text, then a tool call, then more text. We now keep that order on screen.</p>
<p>Merchants do not turn this on. It arrives with the widget. Every channel starts showing cards in place once its bundle is on protocol version 3. An older bundle keeps the cards-last path until the tab reloads.</p>
<p>A typical turn looks like this. The shopper asks a question that should produce a product card, for example a request to see a pair of sneakers and then a recommendation. The first bubble writes a lead-in at reading pace. A shimmer holds a beat, then a product card or carousel takes that place, with its buttons still greyed. Closing text writes in a new bubble under the card. When the turn finishes, the buttons become usable and one feedback control sits under the last piece.</p>
<p><figure class="article-figure"><img src="/cw-draft-13ec83eda14bc2bc/media/interleaved-answer-parts/01-card-in-the-answer.png" alt="A product card in a Crossword conversation, from the current design reference." /><figcaption>A product card in a Crossword conversation, from the current design reference.</figcaption></figure></p>
<h2 id="the-answer-keeps-the-agents-order">The answer keeps the agent's order</h2><p>Each piece is its own bubble, stacked in sequence: text, card, text. A second card and more text after it work the same way. There is no separate cap on cards. The agent&#39;s usual per-answer limits still apply.</p>
<p>This is a stack of bubbles. A single continuous bubble with a card sitting between paragraphs is a later, separate job.</p>
<p>The same order is there after the live turn. Reopen the conversation and the pieces stay where the shopper saw them. The Conversations transcript in the dashboard keeps that order too, so an operator reviewing the exchange sees the lead-in, the card and the closing text as one turn. The playground and model comparison use the same sequence.</p>
<p><figure class="article-figure"><img src="/cw-draft-13ec83eda14bc2bc/media/interleaved-answer-parts/02-conversation-order.png" alt="The Conversations inspect view in the current dashboard design reference." /><figcaption>The Conversations inspect view in the current dashboard design reference.</figcaption></figure></p>
<h2 id="text-at-a-reading-pace-then-a-short-shimmer">Text at a reading pace, then a short shimmer</h2><p>Live text is written out at about 250 characters a second, whichever model is answering. A faster model is typed out at that reading pace. A slower one is shown as it arrives. When the model has finished, the last words simply finish typing.</p>
<p>When a card belongs in the answer, a shimmering placeholder appears in that place and is replaced by the real card. The placeholder stays on screen for at least 0.35 seconds, even if the card is ready sooner, so the change is visible and does not flicker. The slot can wait for up to 30 seconds. If the card never arrives, the slot disappears and the written answer stands on its own.</p>
<p>The thinking placeholder shows only before the first piece. Once a piece has landed, the gap before the next one stays empty. There is no second &quot;Thinking&quot; line and no furniture in that gap.</p>
<h2 id="the-turn-finishes-together">The turn finishes together</h2><p>While the agent is still writing, buttons on a card that has already landed are visibly greyed out. The moment the answer finishes, those buttons become usable. On a later reopen, cards are always usable.</p>
<p>The composer stays locked until the whole answer, including its cards, is finished. If the finished signal is lost, a 60 second watchdog unlocks the box so the shopper is never stuck.</p>
<p>One thumbs-up and thumbs-down control appears under the last piece, whether that piece is text or a card, once the answer is finished. Follow-up chips appear after the last piece. The done sound plays once per answer, on the first piece the shopper can see.</p>
<p>If a card fails to build, the answer text still stands. The shopper sees no card error, only the text. If the answer fails partway after a card has appeared, that card is taken down and the shopper sees the usual error text, never a stray card.</p>
<p>Hidden tabs are unchanged: live frames are not sent to a background tab. Those tabs only ever see finished pieces.</p>
<p>There is no emergency setting to put cards back at the end. Widgets on protocol version 3 show this ordered path. An older bundle keeps cards at the end until the tab reloads.</p>
