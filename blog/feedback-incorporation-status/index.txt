15:T13bd,<p>Merchants can now see what has happened to submitted feedback, and the team can move each report through a status. The reports themselves come from the widget and from people using the extension. How those reports are captured is in the extension-feedback post. This post is about the Widget feedback page in the dashboard, where the team reviews them and updates their status.</p>
<p>The page has a table of reports, with a status column and a filter, and a detail panel for a single report. Team members can show or hide columns, open a report, change its status and leave a comment on the activity timeline. The same page also has a board view, with a column for each status, for teams that prefer to move reports across columns.</p>
<p><figure class="article-figure"><img src="/cw-draft-13ec83eda14bc2bc/media/feedback-incorporation-status/01-feedback-reports-table.png" alt="The Widget feedback table in the current design reference." /><figcaption>The Widget feedback table in the current design reference.</figcaption></figure></p>
<h2 id="a-status-for-every-report">A status for every report</h2><p>Each report has a status the team can update. The shipped statuses are New, Under review, Planned, In progress and Incorporated. Filters can show one status or several at a time, and can also narrow by channel, by a search over email or note, and by a date range.</p>
<p>When a report arrives, and when its status later changes, the reporter can receive an email at the address they typed. Those settings apply to every channel in the workspace. A receipt email can go out as soon as the reporter hits Send. The default is to send that receipt. The default wording for each status is:</p>
<ul>
<li>New: &quot;We received your feedback about {site}. Thanks for letting us know.&quot;</li>
<li>Under review: &quot;Our team is reviewing the issue you reported on {site}.&quot;</li>
<li>Planned: &quot;We plan to fix the issue you reported on {site} in an upcoming update.&quot;</li>
<li>In progress: &quot;We&#39;re working on a fix for the issue you reported on {site}.&quot;</li>
<li>Incorporated: &quot;The issue you reported on {site} has been fixed. Thanks for letting us know.&quot;</li>
</ul>
<p><code>{site}</code> is the host of the reported page. A workspace can edit that wording, choose which status changes send an email and choose whether a receipt email goes out when a report arrives. Team comments on the timeline are only visible to the team.</p>
<h2 id="the-detail-panel-and-the-activity-timeline">The detail panel and the activity timeline</h2><p>Opening a report opens a side panel. The panel holds the description, the images, links into the conversation and the run trace, the runs in that conversation, environment details and a debug timeline of console errors, connection events and widget events. It also holds the status control.</p>
<p>The Activity timeline records when the report was sent, status changes, team comments and whether a reporter email was sent or failed. A team member can add a comment for the team from the same panel.</p>
<p>A team member can update the status from the table, from the board or from the detail panel. Saving a status change writes a timeline event and updates the report immediately.</p>
<p><figure class="article-figure"><img src="/cw-draft-13ec83eda14bc2bc/media/feedback-incorporation-status/02-feedback-report-activity.png" alt="A feedback report's activity timeline and status picker." /><figcaption>A feedback report's activity timeline and status picker.</figcaption></figure></p>
<h2 id="asking-before-the-reporter-is-emailed">Asking before the reporter is emailed</h2><p>By default, a status change that is set to email the reporter asks first. The control is a box labelled &quot;Email the reporter about this change,&quot; ticked by default. In that ask mode, the change waits for Save. A workspace can switch the mode to automatic, so the email goes out as soon as the status changes, with no box.</p>
<p>If the email fails, the failure is recorded on the timeline, with a reason such as email not being set up, the service being unavailable, the address being rejected or the daily limit being reached. The status itself does not change back. A failed email does not undo the move.</p>
<p>Settings also let a workspace name team addresses that should be alerted when a new report arrives, up to ten addresses. Those alerts are separate from the emails the reporter receives.</p>
<p>The table columns a team can show or hide include email, status, description, images, conversation, last run, run status, channel, page URL, widget version and the created date. Image and thumbnail links are short-lived signed addresses, lasting five minutes per request. From the panel, a teammate can also copy the debug JSON, open the conversation and open the run trace. A board view uses the same statuses. This is where submitted reports are reviewed, planned and, when the work is done, marked incorporated, with a record of who moved them and whether the reporter was told.</p>
